3. Four Simple Rules to Reset Our Clock in 3 Days (Zero Burnout)

☀️ 1. No Sunglasses for First 20 Mins Outside (07:00–08:30)

  • Plain-English Why: Your eyes have built-in light sensors connected to your brain's master clock. At 7:30 AM in Tokyo, your brain thinks it is 4:30 PM in Chicago.
  • When bright morning outdoor light hits your eyes at 7:30 AM, it tells your brain "The sun just rose—reset to morning!"
  • What to do: Nothing extra—just leave sunglasses off while walking to breakfast or the train station on Thursday, Friday, and Saturday mornings.

⏱️ 2. Keep Afternoon Power Naps to 25–45 Minutes Max

  • When the 2:30 PM crash hits during our afternoon hotel/lounge break, taking a short power nap feels amazing.
  • Critical Rule: Always set an alarm for 25 to 45 minutes max and follow it with an iced matcha or coffee.
  • If you sleep for 2–3 hours from 2:30 PM to 5:30 PM, you will wake up wide awake at midnight and stay jet-lagged all week.

💊 3. Take 0.5mg–1mg Melatonin at 21:00–21:30 (Nights 1–4)

  • Because 11:00 PM Tokyo = 8:00 AM Chicago, your body will naturally try to wake up around 11:00 PM–Midnight on Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday nights.
  • Taking a low-dose melatonin gummy/tablet (0.5mg–1mg) at 21:00–21:30 keeps your brain in sleep mode past that 11:00 PM spike so you sleep straight through the night.

✈️ 4. Plane Sleep Trick on Wednesday's Flight

  • We land at Haneda at 17:45 Tokyo time and reach our Shinjuku hotel around 21:00.
  • Try to sleep during the first half of the flight and stay awake for the last 4–5 hours before landing so you are genuinely tired at 21:30 Tokyo time on Wednesday night.

4. Practical On-the-Ground Tokyo & Hakone Hacks

📱 Suica in Apple Wallet (Tap & Go)

  • Add a Suica card right inside Apple Wallet before flying and load ¥5,000 with Mastercard or Amex.
  • With Express Transit enabled, you don't even need to unlock your iPhone or open Face ID—just tap your phone on train gates, buses, vending machines, coin lockers, and 7-Eleven registers.

🎒 1-Night Backpack Hack for Hakone (Mon Nov 30)

  • Never haul big rolling suitcases onto the Hakone mountain gondola or Lake Ashi pirate ship!
  • On Monday morning, leave your main suitcases stored at your Shinjuku hotel bell desk (or a station locker) and bring only a light 1-night backpack for your Hakone Ryokan stay and Tuesday's 33-minute Bullet Train back.

💴 Cash vs. Card & Physical Passports

  • 90% Tap-to-Pay: Almost all stores, restaurants, and department halls take Apple Pay / contactless credit cards.
  • Keep ¥10,000–¥15,000 Cash in ¥1,000 Bills: Mountain snack stalls (Mt. Takao dango), temple charms, and a few street stalls are cash-only. Withdraw at any 7-Eleven ATM.
  • Carry Your Physical Passport: Required by law for tourists, gives you instant 10% Tax-Free shopping at stores, and is mandatory (along with your 1949 IDP + physical US driver's license) for Tuesday's Street Go-Karts.

🍱 Quick No-Pork / No-Alcohol Ordering Cheat Sheet

  • 100% Safe Go-Tos: Wagyu beef skewers/teppanyaki, chicken yakitori (salt/shio), shrimp & vegetable tempura, sushi/nigiri, charcoal unagi (eel), buckwheat soba, tori-paitan (chicken broth) ramen, crepes, and matcha/mochi sweets.
  • Watch Out For: Standard tonkotsu ramen (pork bone broth), standard gyoza (usually pork unless labeled chicken/seafood/vegan), menchi-katsu patties, and diced ham inside convenience-store potato/egg salads (use Google Lens camera translate + show the Chef Card on the Checklist page!).
