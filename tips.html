2. Four Simple Rules to Reset Our Clock in 3 Days

☀️ 1. No Sunglasses for First 20 Mins Outside (Thu–Sat)

  • Bright morning outdoor daylight between 07:00–08:30 tells your brain's master clock the sun just rose, shifting you onto Tokyo time twice as fast.

⏱️ 2. Cap Afternoon Power Naps at 30–45 Mins

  • Always set an alarm during your Thu/Sat hotel naps and follow with an iced matcha or coffee so you don't wake up wide awake at midnight.

💊 3. Take 0.5mg–1mg Melatonin at 21:00 (Nights 1–3)

  • 11:00 PM Tokyo = 8:00 AM Chicago. Low-dose melatonin at 21:00 prevents your Chicago body clock from giving you a midnight second wind.

✈️ 4. Plane Sleep Strategy on Wednesday

  • Sleep during the first half of the flight and stay awake for the last 4–5 hours before landing at 17:45 so you sleep deeply on Night 1.

  • 90% Contactless + ¥15,000 Cash: Apple Pay works almost everywhere; keep ¥1,000 bills from a 7-Eleven ATM for mountain stalls & temples.
  • Physical Passport + 1949 IDP + US License: Carry your passport daily (for 10% Tax-Free shopping) and all 3 physical docs for Tuesday's Go-Karts.

🍱 Quick No-Pork / No-Alcohol Ordering Guide

  • Safe Go-Tos: Wagyu beef, chicken yakitori (salt/shio), shrimp/veg tempura, sushi, unagi, buckwheat soba, tori-paitan (chicken broth) ramen, crepes & mochi.
  • Avoid: Pork-bone tonkotsu ramen, standard pork gyoza, and diced ham in convenience-store salads (use Google Lens + the Chef Card on the Checklist page).
