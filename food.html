Quick take

  • Pork is hidden in many Japanese dishes (broth, lard, gelatin), so this list is sorted by how well each pick is documented.
  • 4 picks have venue-published evidence: T's Tantan, Honolu, Panga and Toraya's Yoru no Ume yokan. The rest are marked Confirm first.
  • Show the ordering card (below) before you order. Cooking sake and mirin are fine under your rules.
  • No restaurant here is certified by this research. Always confirm with staff and read labels.

Tiers: A strongest fit, B worth it if it fits, C conditional or lower priority. Ranked using traveler experiences, review volume and agreement across sources, and numbers follow that order within each tier. Prices are approximate JPY per adult.

Show this before you order

Ordering wording. Show this before ordering; it accurately permits cooking sake/mirin and shared equipment:

豚肉は食べられません。豚骨・ポークエキス・ラード・豚由来のゼラチンも食べられません。鶏肉・牛肉・魚介・卵・乳製品は大丈夫です。料理に使う日本酒やみりんは大丈夫ですが、お酒は飲みません。同じ調理器具の使用は問題ありません。

I cannot eat pork, pork bones, pork extract, lard or pork-derived gelatin. Chicken, beef, seafood, eggs and dairy are fine. Sake and mirin used in cooking are fine, but I do not drink alcoholic beverages. Shared cooking equipment is fine.

Follow with:

この料理のスープ、だし、たれ、油、衣に、豚由来の材料は入っていますか。

Do the soup, stock, sauce, oil or batter in this dish contain any pork-derived ingredients?

For chicken, よく火を通してください means "Please cook it thoroughly." Do not describe this preference as a medical allergy. If the kitchen cannot establish ingredients, choose a different dish; asking for visible pork to be removed is insufficient.

  • Ichiran and ordinary tonkotsu ramen: pork-bone broth directly conflicts with the requirement. Omitting chashu does not solve it. No recommendation is made.
  • Tonkatsu, pork gyoza, nikuman and pork-heavy ramen streets: excluded for ingredients or menu fit, not because they lack fans. Likewise, Omoide Yokocho/Golden Gai drinking crawls mismatch the no-alcohol, balanced-day brief.
  • Default CoCo Ichibanya "vegetable curry": vegetable toppings are not the same thing as the separate CoCoICHI vegetarian curry. The official English page explicitly describes the latter as plant-based, 683, at participating stores. Ordinary curry sauce cannot be inferred suitable from its topping. Not shortlisted: much of the standard menu is unsuitable or needs sauce checks; this is a fallback distinction, not an independent quality endorsement.
  • Ginza Kagari as a 1,200-yen guaranteed pork-free bargain: both propositions are unsupported for this trip. The opened 2026 visit reports a 2,000-yen entry bowl; complete broth/fat verification remains missing.
  • Maruka as an automatic "must": genuine popularity does not cancel the lost 30-90 minutes. Reports disagree about the hot noodle texture. Keep it optional, not a scheduled obligation.
  • Tsukiji giant premium seafood snacks and multiple market meals: no verified price advantage was established. Compare posted portion prices with a seated seafood meal and cap impulse spending. The market atmosphere can be worthwhile without buying an expensive skewer.

Booking, weather, safety and what is still unverified

Practical approach. Bincho, Imahan and Sushi Zanmai have official reservation channels. Panga's venue-written TableCheck limits dinner reservations to one month ahead; lunch is walk-in. Honolu's owner says no reservations despite Tabelog's contrary flag. Check fees, minimum orders and service charges. No bookings made; avoid prepaid courses with unresolved ingredients.

Queue and weather discipline. Cool/rainy outdoor waiting may be uncomfortable; no forecast is claimed. Favor indoor meals/food halls in rain, carry an umbrella and consider a 20-30-minute queue cutoff, not a prediction. Recheck weekly closures and last orders.

Package-label checks. Find 原材料名 (ingredients), not just nutrition/allergens. Watch for 豚肉 / ポーク (pork), 豚骨 (pork bones), ポークエキス / 豚エキス (pork extract), ラード / 豚脂 (lard/pork fat), and 豚由来 (pork-derived). ゼラチン is gelatin: ask its source rather than assuming it is pork or assuming it is acceptable. 寒天 is agar. 動物性油脂 (animal fat), ショートニング (shortening) and an unspecified fat blend may require clarification. 乳化剤 means emulsifier; the word alone does not prove pork origin. 牛脂 is beef fat and is not excluded by this brief.

An "includes pork" statement is a useful warning; absence from a short allergen panel is not complete ingredient proof. Cooking sake, mirin, cooking wine or alcohol-related ingredient wording is not automatically disqualifying under the stated drinks-only restriction. Choose water, tea or other ordinary soft drinks rather than inventing a broader alcohol ban.

Food handling. Eat perishable takeaway promptly and follow refrigeration/use-by instructions. Raw fish, raw egg and undercooked chicken cannot be called guaranteed safe. None of the restaurants is certified by this report; shared equipment is not an added restriction, but pork-based frying fat remains an ingredient.
