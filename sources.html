Where the recommendations come from, how much to trust them, and who took the photos. Walks and Food sources were opened and checked on 2026-10-19; Activities and Must-see sources on 2026-10-19/20.

What these sources are (and are not)

  • Walks and Food (iteration 1) rest mostly on firsthand traveler diaries (Travellerspoint-heavy) plus official venue and government pages for hours, closures and prices. Tour marketplaces (Viator, Klook, GetYourGuide), TripAdvisor and Reddit could not be read for them.
  • Activities and Must-see (iteration 2) were redone with Google Maps, TripAdvisor, official venue pages, blogs and, where tours exist, Viator, GetYourGuide and Klook, read through a visible browser. Reddit stayed blocked, so no thread was opened. Google AI summaries were not used as evidence, Klook counts of 1,000 or more are rounded, and Viator and TripAdvisor tour counts are one pool.
  • No video or podcast was actually watched or heard for any category. Rankings do not claim otherwise.
  • The More ideas page is from my own knowledge (to about June 2026), not from these sources. It has no source list and no photos.
  • Review counts are never added across platforms or used as safety scores. For Must-see, Walks and Food they were only checked for some items.
  • Source IDs (like S01) are local to each category. Links on the cards jump to the matching row here.
