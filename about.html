♨️ 2. Hakone Ryokan: Hakone Kowakudani Onsen Ryokan Mizunoto ($380 — Booked ✅)

Booked Room & Hot Springs (Mon Nov 30 – Tue Dec 1)

  • Room: Suikanosho Japanese-Western Room with Private Hinoki Open-Air Balcony Bath (35 m²) — golden Hinoki cypress tub on a mountain-view balcony with twin beds pushed flush together.
  • Bonus Baths & Perks: 3 free outdoor maple-garden private hot-spring huts (no reservation needed—just lock the door from inside) plus free post-bath ice pops, morning probiotic drinks, and 22:30 Yonaki Soba ramen.
  • Backup Status: Backup hold at Susukinohara Ichinoyu ($224) has been canceled ✅.
  • Links: Official Rooms · Balcony Tub Photo · Google Maps

Transit & No-Pork Dining Plan

  • Mon 15:40 Arrival: Take the 18-minute H-Line Bus from Moto-Hakone Port (covered by your Hakone Freepass) to Kowakidani Station, then hop on the free 3-minute hotel shuttle.
  • Mon Dinner: Grab early wagyu or tempura soba in Moto-Hakone or Gora, or pick up salmon rice balls and snacks at the Kowakidani Lawson convenience store before enjoying the hotel's free 22:30 ramen.
  • Tue 10:00 Checkout: Take the 35-minute train or bus down to Odawara Station for the 11:30 AM Bullet Train (arrives at Tokyo Station Ramen Street at 12:03 PM).
