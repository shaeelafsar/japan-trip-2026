3. Our Traveler Profile & Non-Negotiables

🕰️ Chicago-to-Tokyo Sleep & Daily Rhythm

  • In Chicago we naturally sleep ~11:30 PM–12:30 AM to 8:00–8:30 AM CST. In Tokyo (+15 hrs), our eyes naturally pop open around 05:30–06:00 AM on Day 2 (Thu) and shift ~45–60 minutes later each day into a smooth staircase (05:30 Thu → 06:15 Fri → 07:15 Sat → 08:00 Sun → 08:15 Mon → 08:30–09:00 Tue).
  • 1.5–2 Hour Morning Routine Built In: Every day accounts for a relaxed 1.5 to 2 hours to wake up, shower, eat breakfast, and head out naturally—leaving at 07:45 on Thu (for Mt. Takao's 08:00 Cable Car), 08:15 on Fri (Mt. Fuji coach), 09:15 on Sat, and 10:00+ on Sun–Tue.
  • Protected 2:30 PM Recharge Breaks: Every day places a hotel power-nap, seated highway coach, or lounge break right across the 14:00–16:15 window so we never burn out mid-afternoon.

🏔️ What We Enjoy (and What We Don't)

  • Real Landmarks & Scenery: We love iconic places with real presence (Rocky Mountains, Empire State Building, Times Square, Navy Pier, Gatlinburg's Anakeesta) and dislike artificial Instagram/TikTok photo-op rooms.
  • Casual-to-Moderate Trails: We enjoy mountain gondolas, cable cars, and beginner-to-moderate forest/ridge trails (zero rock-climbing or extreme backcountry).
  • Food Style: Strictly no pork and no alcohol. We love clean, upscale prepared food-stall hopping (wagyu skewers, sushi, tempura, ramen, crepes, mochi) and flexible Maps/TikTok dining over rigid reservations.

5. Why Days & Times Are Ordered This Way (Jet-Lag Staircase & Our 4 Optimization Rules)

Optimization Rule How It Shapes & Re-Arranged Our Week
Rule 1: The Chicago → Tokyo (+15 Hr) Jet-Lag Staircase & 2:30 PM Wall • Why 2:30 PM Tokyo Hits Like a Wall: In Chicago we sleep ~11:30 PM–12:30 AM to 8:00–8:30 AM CST. Because Tokyo is +15 hours ahead, 2:30 PM Tokyo = 11:30 PM Chicago bedtime (the exact mid-afternoon crash we felt in Bali), while 05:30–06:30 AM Tokyo = 2:30–3:30 PM Chicago (wide-awake afternoon energy!).
• How Our Wake-Up Shifts (+45–60 Mins/Day): Instead of fighting our body clock with early alarms late in the trip or wasting our early wake-ups on Days 2–3, our schedule steps forward smoothly each morning (05:30 Thu → 06:15 Fri → 07:15 Sat → 08:30 Sun → 08:15 Mon → 08:30–09:00 Tue) with a full 1.5–2 hour morning routine built in before leaving the hotel.
• How Jet Lag Re-Arranged Sat, Sun & Tue:
  1. Thu & Fri (Days 2–3): Uses our effortless early wake-ups for the 08:00 AM Mt. Takao Cable Car (back at the hotel by 13:15 for a 2h 45m nap across the 2:30 PM wall!) and our booked 08:15 AM Mt. Fuji Coach (napping on the booked 16:18 return coach).
  2. Sat Nov 28 (Day 4 — Lighter & Earlier Night): Moved SHIBUYA SKY to 15:30–17:00 (true 16:28 sunset right after a 90-min hotel nap!) and removed night street go-karting from Saturday so we aren't driving in Tokyo traffic while still adjusting—letting us wrap up with casual Ebisu ramen by 20:00.
