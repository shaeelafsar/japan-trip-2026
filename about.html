♨️ 2. Hakone Ryokan: Hakone Kowakudani Onsen Ryokan Mizunoto ($380 — Booked ✅)

Booked Room & Hot Springs (Mon Nov 30 – Tue Dec 1)

  • Room: Suikanosho Japanese-Western Room w/ Private Hinoki Open-Air Balcony Bath (35 m²) — golden Hinoki cypress tub on a mountain-view balcony + twin beds pushed flush together.
  • Bonus Baths & Perks: 3 free outdoor maple-garden private onsen pavilions (inside lock) + free post-bath ice pops, morning probiotic drinks & 22:30 Yonaki Soba ramen.
  • Backup Reminder: Cancel backup hold (Susukinohara Ichinoyu, $224) before Nov 26.
  • Links: Official Rooms · Balcony Tub Photo · Google Maps

Transit & No-Pork Dining Plan

  • Mon 15:20 Arrival: 18-min H-Line Bus from Moto-Hakone Port (covered by Hakone Freepass) to Kowakidani Station + 3-min free shuttle.
  • Mon Dinner: Grab early wagyu/tempura soba in Moto-Hakone or Gora, or pick up salmon onigiri & snacks at Kowakidani Lawson + free 22:30 hotel ramen.
  • Tue 10:00 Checkout: 35-min train/bus down to Odawara Station for the 11:30 AM Bullet Train (arrives Tokyo Station Ramen Street at 12:03 PM).

✂️ 4. What We Explicitly Cut — And Why

Excluded Item Why We Cut It What We Chose Instead
Universal Studios Japan (Osaka) Eats ~1.75 days + 6 hrs on trains + $800+, and USJ has zero high-speed launch coasters. Fuji-Q Highland (Zokkon & Takabisha launch coasters at the base of Mt. Fuji) + 33-min Bullet Train from Odawara.
teamLab Borderless / Planets Indoor digital projection rooms built for social-media photo-ops. Hands-on cultural bookings (Samurai Museum, Kimono + Tea Ceremony, Sushi Class, Live Sumo, Night Go-Karts).
Tsukiji Outer Market Crowded early-morning wet-fish alleys that close by 14:00. Spotless indoor food halls (Tokyo Station Ramen Street & Gransta, Isetan Depachika) + Omoide Yokocho.
Chureito Pagoda (398 Steps) 398 steep stairs to wait in a crowded photo line. Mt. Fuji Panoramic Ropeway (3-min gondola to Mt. Tenjo summit deck).
Extra Shrines & Museums Avoids temple fatigue on a 1-week trip. Capped at Tokyo's top 2: Meiji Jingu (forest shrine) & Senso-ji (645 AD Buddhist temple).
