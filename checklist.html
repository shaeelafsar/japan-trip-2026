🏆 Why We Chose JR-East Hotel Mets Shibuya

  • Comfortable Room Size & Deep Soaking Tub: Unlike Tokyo's cramped 11–14 m² micro-hotels (APA, Villa Fontaine, Lyf, or Tokyu Stay base rooms), our room gives us 16–19 m² (172–204 sq ft) of uncluttered space, a full modular bathroom with a deep soaking tub + bath salts, an in-room empty refrigerator, electric kettle, and individual AC/heat control.
  • The "2 Streets Over From Waikiki Strip" Sweet Spot: Located in the upscale, spotless Shibuya New South Gate / Shibuya Stream pocket—100% separated from Dogenzaka ("Love Hotel Hill"), Center-Gai, and Kabukicho bar/tout alleys—while keeping us a flat 2-min walk to Shibuya Stream and 8.5-min walk to Shibuya Scramble Crossing & SHIBUYA SKY.
  • Built-In Morning Perks: Features Tully's Coffee + 7-Eleven right on the 1st floor, free coffee/tea + a shared microwave in the 3F lobby lounge, and optional ¥1,980 (~$13) same-day breakfast buffet coupons sold at the front desk whenever we want a sit-down buffet.
  • 7-Night Hakone Suitcase Hack: Keeping our room across Mon Nov 30 means our big suitcases stay locked inside our room while we take a light 1-night backpack to our Hakone Ryokan!

✨ What Our Neighborhood Is Like (Day & Night Vibe)

  • What It Feels Like: Think Waikiki's modern riverwalk + Chicago's Riverwalk/Fulton Market right next to Tokyo's Times Square. The south-east side of Shibuya Station was recently rebuilt into sleek glass-and-greenery pedestrian decks overlooking the Shibuya River.
  • Daytime Vibe: Bright, clean, tree-lined walkways connecting our hotel directly to Shibuya Stream, Shibuya Scramble Square, Miyashita Rooftop Park, Cat Street, and Omotesando without ever having to cross chaotic intersections.
  • Late-Night Vibe (8:30–10:30 PM): Warm lantern and string-light riverfront terraces at Shibuya Stream and Shibuya Sakura Stage (2–4 min walk) filled with couples, cafés, dessert bars, bakeries, sushi, and ramen—plus the giant neon video screens of Shibuya Scramble Crossing 8 minutes up the promenade, and a calm, safe 2-minute stroll back to sleep.
