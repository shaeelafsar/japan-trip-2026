Mon Nov 30

Universal Studios Japan (Osaka)

Early start · Osaka

Shinkansen 06:30 (or Sun night) · USJ ~09:45 to 18:30 · back ~22:30

Before you go: USJ is in Osaka, not Tokyo: about 3 to 3.5 hours each way from Shinjuku (~¥29,000 return per adult). Why Mon Nov 30? It skips weekend crowds, uses the Monday when Tokyo's museum and Shinjuku Gyoen close, and catches late-November Osaka averages (15.9 °C high, 8.4 °C low, rain ~1 day in 5, sunset 16:47) before December turns 2 °C colder. Weather vs Express Pass trade-off: Express Passes guarantee Super Nintendo World and cut 2+ hour lines, but are date-fixed and sell out weeks ahead. If dry sunny weather matters more than Express Passes, wait for the ~Nov 20 forecast and swap Mon Nov 30 with Fri Nov 27 (Fuji) or Tue Dec 1.

More

Daylight, weather and packing
  • Sunrise ~06:30, sunset ~16:30, dark by about 17:00 in Tokyo and Takao. In Osaka (Mon Nov 30) sunrise is 06:45 and sunset is 16:47 (18 minutes more afternoon light than Tokyo). Plan mountain trails to finish before 15:00.
  • Tokyo averages 15 °C high and 7 °C low in late November, 13 and 5 °C in early December. Osaka averages 15.9 °C high and 8.4 °C low in late November, 13.9 and 6.7 °C in early December (Japan Meteorological Agency 1991 to 2020 normals, not a forecast). Cold snaps happen.
  • Rain: in Tokyo about 1 day in 4 in late November and 1 in 6 in early December; in Osaka about 1 day in 5 in November (21%) and 1 in 6 in December (18%) (days with 1 mm or more, JMA 1991 to 2020 normals).
  • Day trips are colder. Mt Takao's summit is about 3 °C below Tokyo. Fuji's lakes run about 3 °C colder by day and 6 to 7 °C colder at night, near freezing. Osaka Bay (USJ) has coastal wind off the water after dark.
  • Autumn colour (Japan Meteorological Corporation forecast, Oct 1; it updates in early November): Tokyo ginkgo about Nov 26 and maple about Nov 28. Takao peaks about Nov 22 and fades through Nov 29. Rikugien peaks about Dec 2. Fuji's lake colour is likely over.
  • Fuji views are a bet. Cold mornings improve the odds but cloud often hides it, and snow or ice can close the 5th Station road. I found no honest percentage.
  • Pack: layers, a windproof and waterproof shell, warm gloves (for evening karting and outdoor coasters), a hat, a compact umbrella and grippy shoes for Takao.
  • Rush hour peaks 08:00 to 09:00 and after 17:00. The Sun, Mon and Tue early starts hit it, so travel light those days.
Where to stay (not booked)

Shinjuku, south or west side (South, New South or West exits), not Kabukicho or Golden Gai.

  • Takao (Keio Line from Shinjuku) and the Fuji tour pickups leave from here, and Shinagawa (for the Osaka bullet train) is 20 minutes away on the JR Yamanote Line.
  • Optional 1-night Universal City stay (Sun Nov 29 or Mon Nov 30): if you want to skip the 05:45 Monday wake-up for USJ, ride the Shinkansen to Osaka on Sunday evening after sumo (~19:30) and book one night at a Universal City hotel 5 minutes from the park gate.
  • Shinjuku Gyoen, department stores and plenty of restaurants are nearby.
  • Shibuya, Harajuku and Ginza are 7 to 16 minutes by train. Haneda is about 60 to 75 minutes.
  • For Option C, stay near Tokyo Station instead.
Weather and swap rules
  • How to get the best weather for USJ (Mon Nov 30) and Fuji (Fri Nov 27): climate normals say late November in Osaka (15.9 / 8.4 °C) is 2 °C warmer than early December (13.9 / 6.7 °C) and drier than Tokyo. If you buy USJ Express Passes now, your USJ date is locked to Mon Nov 30 (many major rides—Mario Kart, Forbidden Journey, indoor shows—are indoors if it rains). If dry sunny weather matters more than Express Passes, wait until ~Nov 20 to check the 7-day forecast for Osaka and Fuji across Fri Nov 27, Mon Nov 30 and Tue Dec 1, put USJ on the sunniest Osaka weekday, and arrive at the gate by 08:00 for a free app timed-entry ticket to Super Nintendo World.
  • Closed days fix the order. Shinjuku Gyoen and the Tokyo National Museum close Mon Nov 30, while USJ is open every day—making Monday the cleanest swap.
  • Pacing: Fri to Tue are five full days in a row (early Fri, Sun and Mon; long Sat and Tue). Tue Dec 1 now includes a 14:00 to 15:45 hotel rest after Monday's Osaka day before sundown/night karting. If energy drops, cut in this order: Sat Ueno Park stroll, Tue Marunouchi lights, or sleep in Osaka on Sun or Mon night.
  • Rain on Sunday (Takao): swap Takao for Shinjuku Gyoen or indoor stops; keep USJ on whichever weekday has the best Osaka forecast.
  • Autumn colour is a bonus, not a reason to move a day. Forecasts shift, so recheck in early November.
  • Kintsugi was cut for karting on Tue Dec 1. Book it instead if karting falls through: Kintsugi workshops.
Tea farms: what I found

Late November is off-season for picking. Operators put picking in May to October. No page says the plants are dormant now, so expect farm visits and tastings, not picking.

  • Sanopy half-day (the swap on Thu 26): GetYourGuide or Viator. 4 hours, about $76 each, Nov 26 and 29 only.
  • Shizuoka private tasting: Viator. 7 hours, from $271 per person, every date Nov 25 to Dec 2. Meet 9:00 at Shizuoka Station (the train is extra). Moriuchi family farm plus Nihondaira views. 10 reviews, 100 percent recommend. Pick a pork-free lunch.
  • Private car tour to the Fuji tea fields: GetYourGuide. 10 hours, about $416 per group of up to 6, hotel pickup around 07:00. Fields are seen from the road only. Dec 1 and 2 were not confirmed. New listing, no ratings.
  • Standard Fuji bus tours have no tea-field stop. The ones I opened offer only a 10 to 45 minute matcha session at a shop.
  • Blocked sources: Viator, GetYourGuide and Klook block automated reading, so I read these pages in a browser on Oct 20. Prices and slots change, so recheck before booking.
Option B: a night in Hakone (Mon 30 to Tue 1)

Replaces Universal Studios Japan on Mon 30. Hakone is a sightseeing loop: ropeway, lake cruise, museum and onsen. It is colder than Tokyo by 4 to 7 °C.

  • Mon: Odakyu Romancecar from Shinjuku (Freepass ¥7,100, or ¥9,500 with Romancecar). Ropeway, Lake Ashi cruise, Open-Air Museum, onsen at the inn.
  • The old Tokaido cedar avenue in Hakone is a busy walk worth adding if the weather is dry. I did not verify its current path conditions.
  • Tue: back by about 13:00, then Senso-ji, sundown karting and dinner near Ginza. The sushi class and Samurai museum drop out.
  • The ropeway can stop for wind or gas. Ask the inn for a pork-free dinner.
Option C: add Kyoto and Nara to the Osaka trip (Sun 29 to Tue 1)

Since Mon Nov 30 already goes to Osaka for USJ (~¥29,000 return by Shinkansen), staying two nights in Osaka or Kyoto lets you add Kyoto and Nara without paying a second bullet-train fare.

  • Sun: morning Shinkansen, Fushimi Inari in the afternoon (start the summit loop before 14:00; sunset is ~16:46), then Kiyomizu-dera night viewing (to Nov 30).
  • Mon: Universal Studios Japan all day.
  • Tue: morning in Kyoto or Nara (Todai-ji and the park), afternoon Shinkansen back to Tokyo for sundown karting or dinner near Ginza.
  • Best match for combining USJ with autumn colour. Costs about two Tokyo days: Mt Takao + sumo on Sun and the Asakusa classes on Tue morning.
Evenings: safety and getting home
  • Every night ends in a busy, well-lit area with a direct train home.
  • Sun (sumo and sushi) is at Okubo, the edge of Kabukicho. Go straight to the venue and decline bar invitations.
  • Tue (sundown/night karting in Shibuya): evening traffic is busy and glare is stronger after dark; follow the guide closely and wear warm gloves.
  • Skip Shibuya's nightlife streets and Roppongi street level. The UK Foreign Office warns about touts and drink spiking there.
Ordering card: no pork, no alcohol

Show this at restaurants. Written by me, not checked by a Japanese speaker: have one confirm it before you print it.

  • 豚肉は食べられません。 (I cannot eat pork.)
  • 豚エキス、ラード、豚骨スープもだめです。 (No pork extract, lard or pork-bone soup.)
  • お酒は飲みません。料理にも酒やみりんを使わないでください。 (I do not drink alcohol. Please do not use sake or mirin in the cooking.)
  • No restaurant is certified pork-free. Broths and fryer oil are the usual hidden sources.
