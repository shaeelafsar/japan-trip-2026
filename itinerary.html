Tea farm swap (optional)

Sanopy tea farm Nov 26 or 29 only

Replaces Meiji Jingu and the matcha class. 4 hours, about $76 each, English guide, field walk, tasting and farm lunch (ask for no pork). Meets at Shin-Matsuda Station, about 1 hour from Shinjuku. More

Daylight, weather and packing
  • Sunrise ~06:30, sunset ~16:30, dark by 17:00. Plan wooded or outdoor stops before 15:30. Evenings are for lights, shows and dinner.
  • Expect 12 to 16 °C by day and 5 to 9 °C at night (my estimate from Japan Guide's monthly averages). Colder on rooftops and the coast.
  • Rain: about 20 to 25 percent of days in late November, 10 to 15 percent in early December.
  • Pack: layers, a windproof shell, a compact umbrella, gloves and a hat.
  • Rush hour peaks 08:00 to 09:00 and after 17:00. The 9:00 starts hit it, so travel light those days.
Where to stay (not booked)

Shinjuku, south or west side (South, New South or West exits), not Kabukicho or Golden Gai.

  • Kamakura, Hakone and the Fuji tour pickups all leave from here.
  • Tsunahachi, Imahan, Isetan and Sushi Zanmai are nearby.
  • Shibuya, Harajuku and Ginza are 7 to 16 minutes by train. Haneda is about 60 to 75 minutes.
  • For Option C, stay near Tokyo Station instead.
Weather and swap rules
  • Fuji is the flex day. Check the forecast about a week out. A guided tour is easier to move or cancel only if its terms allow it, so read them before you book.
  • Closed days fix the order. Shinjuku Gyoen and the Tokyo National Museum close Mon Nov 30. Shinjuku's south observatory closes Tue Dec 1.
  • Both days wet: keep the indoor items (museum, teamLab, classes, Isetan) and do Kamakura's temples in rain gear.
  • Autumn colour peaks late November to early December in Tokyo. Do not build a day around it.
Tea farms: what I found

Late November is off-season for picking. Operators put picking in May to October. No page says the plants are dormant now, so expect farm visits and tastings, not picking.

  • Sanopy half-day (the swap on Thu 26): GetYourGuide or Viator. 4 hours, about $76 each, Nov 26 and 29 only.
  • Shizuoka private tasting: Viator. 7 hours, from $271 per person, every date Nov 25 to Dec 2. Meet 9:00 at Shizuoka Station (the train is extra). Moriuchi family farm plus Nihondaira views. 10 reviews, 100 percent recommend. Pick a pork-free lunch.
  • Private car tour to the Fuji tea fields: GetYourGuide. 10 hours, about $416 per group of up to 6, hotel pickup around 07:00. Fields are seen from the road only. Dec 1 and 2 were not confirmed. New listing, no ratings.
  • Standard Fuji bus tours have no tea-field stop. The ones I opened offer only a 10 to 45 minute matcha session at a shop.
  • Blocked sources: Viator, GetYourGuide and Klook block automated reading, so I read these pages in a browser on Oct 20. Prices and slots change, so recheck before booking.
Option B: a night in Hakone (Mon 30 to Tue 1)

Replaces Kamakura. No unguided hiking: Hakone is ropeway, lake cruise, museum and onsen.

  • Mon: Odakyu Romancecar from Shinjuku (Freepass ¥7,100, or ¥9,500 with Romancecar). Ropeway, Lake Ashi cruise, Open-Air Museum, onsen at the inn.
  • Tue: back by about 13:00, then Senso-ji, kintsugi and the Bincho dinner. Sushi class and the Samurai museum drop out.
  • The ropeway can stop for wind or gas. Ask the inn for a pork-free dinner.
Option C: two nights in Kyoto and Nara (Sun 29 to Tue 1)

Keeps Thu to Sat as planned. Costs about ¥28,000 to 31,000 per adult by Shinkansen (2 h 15 min each way), plus two nights.

  • Sun: Fushimi Inari in the afternoon, then Kiyomizu-dera night viewing (to Nov 30). Skip the summit loop; stay on the main gates.
  • Mon: Nara for Todai-ji and the park, back to Kyoto for the evening.
  • Tue: Kyoto morning, train back, then Ginza eel and the Marunouchi lights.
  • Best match for autumn colour. Costs about three Tokyo days.
Evenings: safety and getting home
  • Every night ends in a busy, well-lit area with a direct train home.
  • Sun (sumo and sushi) is at Okubo, the edge of Kabukicho. Go straight to the venue and decline bar invitations.
  • Skip Shibuya's nightlife streets and Roppongi street level. The UK Foreign Office warns about touts and drink spiking there.
Left out, and why
  • Mt Fuji on your own, Chureito alone, the Kamakura forest trail: you asked for guides on uncertain terrain.
  • Mt Takao, Mitake, Oyama: bear alerts.
  • Skytree deck, Nikko, snow monkeys, Sanrio Puroland, Fuji-Q: weak fit for these dates.
  • Ghibli Museum: closed Dec 1.
  • Swap pool: teamLab Planets, Rikugien illumination, Hamarikyu, Akihabara, Sumida riverside, Shinjuku Gyoen, Isehiro and Kanda Yabusoba lunches.
