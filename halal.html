✅ Category 1: 100% Halal-Certified Meat Restaurants

  • Zero Guessing: Every spot labeled "✅ 100% Halal-Certified Meat" (Gyumon Shibuya, Shinjuku-tei across Shibuya/Shinjuku/Harajuku/Asakusa, Honolu Ebisu, Yakiniku no Mikore Shinjuku, THE SLICE Omotesando, Tendon Ginza Itsuki, Yakiniku PANGA, CoCo Ichibanya Halal Akihabara, Mrs Istanbul Haneda T3) uses certified Halal beef/chicken and zero pork or alcohol in its broths and sauces.

🐟🥬 Category 2: Naturally Halal Seafood & Vegetarian Spots

  • Why It Works So Well in Japan: Traditional Japanese Soba (buckwheat noodles), Tempura (shrimp, squid, fish & vegetables fried in sesame/vegetable oil), Sushi, Houtou (pumpkin miso flat noodles), and Unagi (charcoal-grilled freshwater eel) use kelp (kombu) and bonito fish flake (katsuo) dashi broth—100% free of pork or meat!
  • When Ordering Seafood/Veg: Simply show the Japanese card below so staff confirm there is no pork extract/bacon and guide you to fish/kelp dashi or salt-seasoned dishes.
🪪 Show This Screen to Waitstaff (No Pork & No Alcohol — Seafood, Veg, Halal Beef/Chicken OK)
恐れ入りますが、豚肉（ベーコン・ハム・豚骨出汁・ラード等）とアルコール（お酒・みりん）を控えております。魚介類（海鮮・天ぷら・そば・うなぎ）・野菜料理、または豚肉を含まない牛肉・鶏肉料理をご案内いただけますと幸いです。ありがとうございます！
"Excuse us for the trouble, but we kindly refrain from pork (bacon, ham, pork broth, lard, etc.) and alcohol (sake/mirin). We would be so grateful if you could guide us to seafood (sushi, tempura, soba, unagi), vegetable dishes, or beef/chicken dishes that contain no pork. Thank you very much!"
